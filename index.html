Your travel plan

Four days in Vagamon

The timings below are suggested sightseeing slots, not guaranteed attraction opening hours. Adjust them to your starting city, road conditions, weather and your family's pace.

01

Arrival, tea gardens & viewpoint

Friday · Check in and take it easy
MorningStart your journey to Vagamon. Travel time depends on your starting city.
12:00–1:00 PMLunch on the way or after reaching the area.
1:00–2:30 PMReach your accommodation, check in and rest.
3:00–4:00 PMTake a relaxed drive through nearby tea plantations.
4:00–5:30 PMVisit Karikadu View Point, if time and weather permit.
EveningReturn to your stay, have dinner and relax.
Family tip: Keep the first day light after the journey. If you arrive late, skip the viewpoint and enjoy the resort.
02

Vagamon's signature attractions

Saturday · Pine forest, adventure park & meadows
7:00–8:00 AMBreakfast at your accommodation.
8:30–10:00 AMExplore Vagamon Pine Forest and enjoy the tall trees and photo spots.
10:15 AM–12:30 PMVisit the Vagamon adventure area and glass bridge, if open and suitable for your family.
12:30–1:30 PMLunch.
1:30–2:30 PMReturn to your accommodation and rest.
3:00–5:30 PMVisit Vagamon Meadows (Mottakunnu). Enjoy the open hills and late-afternoon scenery.
EveningReturn to your stay for dinner.
Best photo time: Late afternoon at the meadows can be lovely, although mist and rain may reduce visibility.
03

Hill viewpoints & optional waterfall

Sunday · A flexible sightseeing day
7:00–8:00 AMBreakfast.
8:00–9:00 AMVisit Thangal Para.
9:15–10:00 AMVisit Murugan Mala.
10:15–11:30 AMVisit Kurisumala, if your family is comfortable with the walk and terrain.
11:30 AM–12:30 PMEnjoy a scenic drive through the tea plantations.
12:30–1:30 PMLunch.
1:30–3:30 PMOptional visit to Marmala Waterfalls, only if access, weather and road conditions are suitable.
4:00 PM onwardsReturn to Vagamon, relax and enjoy a final family dinner.
Safety note: Waterfall paths can be steep and slippery, especially after rain. Skip Marmala if travelling with elderly members, young children or anyone with mobility concerns.
04

Check out & return journey

Monday · Keep the morning simple
6:00–7:00 AMWake up and have breakfast, or arrange a packed breakfast with your stay.
7:00–8:00 AMCheck out and load your luggage.
8:00 AM onwardsStart your return journey.
Avoid adding a major attraction on Monday so the family can start the return journey without rushing.

Before you go

Family travel tips

Pack layersCarry light jackets or sweaters for cool mornings and evenings.
Wear suitable shoesChoose comfortable shoes with grip for forest paths and viewpoints.
Check the weatherHill fog and rain can change visibility, access and driving times.
Confirm attraction detailsCheck current opening hours, entry fees, glass bridge operation and waterfall access locally.
Choose a convenient stayLook around Vagamon town or Kolahalamedu to reduce backtracking.
Keep the plan flexiblePrioritise the meadows and pine forest; treat the other stops as optional.
